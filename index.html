About Achal Artworks

Achal Artworks was created to bring high-quality, reusable, and vibrant traditional artwork to modern Indian homes. Our pieces are handcrafted with durable acrylic bases, bright premium felt, glitters, and mirrors, offering a sustainable alternative to traditional powder-based rangolis.

Designed with storage in mind, our art sets can be packed away and reused year after year for Diwali, Ganeshotsav, weddings, and all festive celebrations.
